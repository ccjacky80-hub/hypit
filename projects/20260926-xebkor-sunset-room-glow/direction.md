## Core concept

**One Astronaut, New Room.** A creator aims the small astronaut lamp at an empty wall. A small circle of light becomes a room-sized amber-and-rose sunset, changing the emotional character of the exact same bedroom. The light retracts into the astronaut's circular lamp face, returning to the first frame.

## Directing priorities

- Preserve one cast, outfit, room, lamp, stand, and wall across all shots.
- The circular glow and its center point are the continuity handoff from S03 to S04.
- Let the payoff stay clean: no product card, technical specifications, or captions.
- Keep the resulting room believable: light falls on real bedding, wall, and furniture rather than becoming a fantasy landscape.
