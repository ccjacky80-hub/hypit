## Core concept

**Wall-to-Cinema Match Transformation.** The creator presents the projector, places it toward a blank wall and presses power. The rectangle of light expands into a golden coastline cinema world. The world collapses into the projector lens and returns to the opening pose.

## Directing priorities

- Treat the product reference images as factual input for body shape, lens side, vent placement, and white/black color blocking. Do not ask models to render a readable logo.
- Keep a single cast, outfit, bedroom, side table, and wall position across the film.
- The rectangle and horizon geometry are the handoff between S03 and S04; protect them more than incidental scenic detail.
- The camera crosses the wall only once, at 5.2 seconds. Let the payoff breathe without cards or specifications.
