# Generation Runbook

## Preconditions

1. Confirm the provider/account scope and accepted spend before any paid request.
2. Add rights-cleared product images to `assets/reference/product/`.
3. Generate I01 and I02 with Jimeng; select only one consistent cast/room/device identity.
4. Do not run S01–S05 until a human operator is ready to use Google Flow.

## Order and continuity

| Step | Request | Required carry-forward |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jimeng I01 | Product image controls physical device facts. |
| 2 | Jimeng I02 | I01 controls cast/room; product images control device. |
| 3 | Flow S01 | I02 is first-frame/identity reference. |
| 4 | Flow S02 | S01 final frame and product reference. |
| 5 | Flow S03 | Exact S02 final frame. |
| 6 | Flow S04 | Exact S03 final frame, especially wall rectangle/horizon. |
| 7 | Flow S05 | S04 end plus I02/S01 opening to create the loop. |

## Selection rules

Select for continuity before spectacle. A beautiful clip that changes the projector lens side, makes the device float, or loses the wall geometry is rejected. Keep the accepted output identifiers in `STATUS.md` after each choice.

## Local edit

Assemble to 14.0 seconds at 30 fps. Add audio only after the visual loop is accepted. Keep music commercial-use cleared and keep the affiliate disclosure in the publishing context.
